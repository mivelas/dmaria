Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I place my request?

Because we never mass-produce and only accept a limited number of orders per day, we recommend at least 3 to 7 days in advance. For large milestones or weddings, 2 to 4 weeks is advised.

Can I customize the flavor, sweetness, or decoration?

Absolutely. That is the soul of d'María. We can adjust the sweetness level, accommodate flavor twists (like adding sea salt caramel or espresso notes), and tailor custom inscriptions and hand-piped finishes.

Do you accommodate food allergies?

We take dietary restrictions very seriously. Please note all allergies in your request. While we can prepare nut-free and lower-sugar recipes, please be aware our boutique kitchen also handles dairy, wheat flour, and nuts.

How does payment and confirmation work?

After reviewing your request, we send you a clear quote via your preferred contact channel (WhatsApp or Email). A 50% deposit confirms your date on our baking schedule, with the balance settled upon pickup or delivery.
